## Overview

QMetry for Jira is a test management platform that integrates directly into Jira, allowing QA teams to manage test cases, test cycles, and test execution without leaving the Jira environment. It is offered by SmartBear as part of its test management portfolio alongside Zephyr.

## Features

- Manual test management within Jira
- Cross-project reporting and dashboard gadgets
- BDD (Behavior-Driven Development) support
- Data residency options for compliance
- No-code test automation capabilities
- Traceability across requirements, test cases, and execution results
- Test versioning and shared test data/parameters
- Hierarchical folders for test asset organization
